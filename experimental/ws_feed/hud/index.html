How We Skim Harder & Beat Competitors

                        

                            1. Scrape everything: Real-time book + tx stream → profiles of every active maker (spreads they actually post, cancel velocity, size behavior).

                            2. Pressure as signal: Low pressure (wide/defensive competitors) = blend lower vol into A-S → tighter reservation inside book → we quote more, they don't.

                            3. Observed spread: Use what competitors are willing to show as "real market" instead of thin on-chain L1. A-S optimal spread competes against reality.

                            4. Historical depth: Replay long-run data with injected competitor profiles from the 0-quote cycles. Measure presence lift and realized bps vs hard-gate baseline.

                            5. No extra guards: All data improves A-S inputs (vol, liquidity, adverse proxy). Final decision = reservation inside the fresh WS book.

                            Result: Higher time-on-touch when edge exists, better total skim, while A-S math keeps toxic low. We outlast and out-skim because we see what they are doing and the math acts on it.
